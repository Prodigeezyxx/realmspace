# Go-to-market — Year 1

## The strategic insight

We are not selling measurement software. We are selling **closing the loop
on physical brand experiences** to people who already trust us to design and
build those experiences. That changes everything about how we sell.

## Three motions, sequenced

### Motion 1 — Embedded (months 0–6)

RealmSpace ships inside every activation Yourself Creative delivers.

- **No new sales motion required.** The agency's existing client conversations
  add a line item: "Measurement & Replay — $4,800 — includes live dashboard,
  3D twin, client report."
- **Margin uplift on existing work.** A $250K booth grows to $254.8K with
  the measurement bundle, and the marginal cost to us is ~$200 (compute +
  energy on the laptop we ship anyway).
- **Case studies generate naturally.** Every activation we run produces a
  reusable demo asset.

**Target: 6 paid embedded deployments in months 1–6.**

### Motion 2 — Partner agencies (months 4–12)

Once we have 3 case studies and the kit is hardened, we approach 10 peer
agencies with a white-label offering.

- Pricing: $2,500 / activation revenue share + $400 / month base.
- Pitch: *"You can offer measurement to your clients without building a CV
  team. We ship the kit, you ship the booth, your name on the report."*
- Distribution: warm intros via the experiential industry network +
  conference presence (BizBash, Event Marketer Awards, C2 Montreal).

**Target: 2 partner agencies signed by month 9, 5 by month 12.**

### Motion 3 — Direct brand pilots (months 6–12)

Hand-picked premium brands with their own experiential calendars and
in-house teams (Nike House of Innovation, LVMH activations, Spotify Wrapped
events). Higher ASP, longer sales cycle, bigger logos.

- Pricing: Pavilion ($14,500) or Campaign (custom, $40K–$120K).
- Pitch: *"You're spending $1.8M on the flagship and you don't know which
  zone converted. We can change that for next quarter."*
- Distribution: cold outbound to heads of brand experience + warm intros
  via the partner agency network we built in Motion 2.

**Target: 2 direct brand pilots signed by month 12.**

## The 4-week pilot sales cycle

For any motion, the cycle compresses to:

| Week | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery call · share Loom demo · qualify activation date | Founder |
| 2 | Onsite recce or virtual walkthrough · zone sketch · contract | Founder + ops |
| 3 | Kit ships · onsite calibration day | Ops |
| 4 | Activation runs · live dashboard · 24h report turnaround | Ops + founder |

If we can't get from first email to signed pilot in 4 weeks, the pitch is
wrong.

## Lead gen — month 1 (validation sprint)

**The 48-hour pre-build experiment** (before writing more code, run this):

1. Friday — record a 90-second Loom walking through the dashboard, the
   twin, the ask-the-room, and the report. Total time: 4 hours.
2. Friday → Sunday — push to a landing page (`realmspace.io`) with three
   pricing tiers and a "Book a pilot" CTA. Total time: 6 hours.
3. Sunday → Monday — $500 of LinkedIn ads targeting *"Head of Experiential
   Marketing"*, *"Brand Experience Director"*, *"Activation Producer"* in
   the US and UK. Run for 5 days.
4. In parallel — 50 personal cold emails to specific people at agencies
   and brands. Template below.

**Pass criteria for the experiment:** 5 booked demo calls within 7 days.
If we hit that, we're funded and we keep building. If we don't, we re-position.

## What we don't sell (Year 1)

- One-off licenses. Always tied to an activation.
- Long-term venue installs. Wrong shape — we're optimising for project, not
  retainer. Revisit at H2.
- Custom CV models. Stay opinionated and let YOLO + VLM do the work.
- White-label software-only. The kit is part of the brand. Sell the whole
  thing or nothing.
